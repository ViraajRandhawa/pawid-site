Delete your Snoutbook account

This page exists so you can request deletion without needing the app installed. Google Play requires it; we would rather you use the in-app route, which is immediate.

The fastest way — in the app

  1. Open Snoutbook and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Read what will and will not be removed, then type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion happens straight away. You may be asked to sign in again first — that is a security check to prove the account is yours.

If you cannot use the app

Email viraajrandhawa27@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We will confirm and complete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your user record and profile details, including your display name.
  • Your block list.
  • Your sign-in credentials — your Google/Firebase authentication record is removed and you can no longer sign in.

What stays, and why

You are shown this in the app before you confirm, not after. Two things deliberately survive:

  1. Dogs you registered stay — with your name removed. A dog is a community identity, not your personal content. If a dog's profile vanished when its registrant left, the neighbourhood would lose the dog. The attribution changes to “Registered by a neighbor”; nothing links the profile to you afterwards.
  2. The text of your chat messages stays, with your name removed. Deleting message bodies would tear holes in other people's conversations. If you need specific messages gone, delete them yourself before deleting your account, or email us and we will remove them.

Neither of these remains attached to your identity. Once your account is gone, there is no way to trace them back to you.

Retention

Deletion is immediate for the records listed above. Routine encrypted backups held by our infrastructure provider may retain copies for up to 30 days before they age out.
