The short version

PawID gives community dogs — especially India's street dogs — a lasting identity, so a dog someone names today can be recognised by someone else tomorrow.

  • Dogs are public. People are not. Dog profiles are visible to everyone using PawID. Your personal details are not.
  • We collect the minimum we need: an account, the photos you take of dogs, and a rough area (about a 5 km square) — never your precise location, and never a history of where you have been.
  • We do not track you, show you ads, or sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

How location works — precisely

This is the part people most often get wrong about apps like this, so here it is in detail:

  • If you allow location, we convert your position into a geohash of precision 5 — a grid cell roughly 5 km across — and keep only that cell.
  • Your exact coordinates are never stored and never leave your device in usable form.
  • The area attached to a scan is used only to rank candidate dogs at the moment of the scan, and is then discarded. It is not saved.
  • We keep no location history for any person.
  • Photos are stripped of EXIF metadata (including any embedded GPS) on your device, before upload.
  • Location is optional. Decline it and everything still works; matching is slightly less precise.

Photos

  • Photos you take or choose are uploaded to Google Cloud Storage and sent to our recognition service to find the dog in the frame and produce a mathematical “fingerprint” (an embedding) used for matching.
  • Dog profile photos are public — that is the point of the product: anyone who meets that dog should be able to recognise it.
  • A photo you take to scan a dog is sent to our recognition service to produce a match, and is not published to any dog's profile.
  • Please photograph the dog, not people. We ask you to fill the frame with the dog. If a person is incidentally in a photo you upload for a dog profile, that photo is public. If you upload something you shouldn't have, contact us and we will remove it.
  • We may use photos submitted to PawID to improve the accuracy of our dog-recognition models. We do not use them for any other purpose, and we do not sell them.

Chat rooms

PawID has neighbourhood chat rooms. Anything you post is visible to everyone in that room. Do not post personal information about yourself or anyone else. Display names are not verified, so treat identity in chat with the same caution as any public forum. You can delete your own messages, block other users, and report messages to us.

Who else is involved

PawID runs on Google Firebase and Google Cloud (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Run), which process data on our behalf as our service providers. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's own privacy terms. If you sign in with Google, Google Sign-In is used for authentication.

We use no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party trackers.

Deleting your data

Full detail, including exactly what is removed and what remains, is on the account deletion page.

You can delete your account from inside the app (Settings → Delete account) or by emailing viraajrandhawa27@gmail.com.

Children

PawID is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Google Cloud. Access to dog and user records is enforced by server-side security rules. No system is perfectly secure; PawID is an early-stage product run by a small team, and you should not put anything in it you would be harmed by seeing in public.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Email viraajrandhawa27@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data behaviour changes and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
