Common questions

The app didn't recognise my dog. Is it broken?

Probably not. Snoutbook can only recognise dogs somebody has already registered — an unregistered dog cannot be identified, by us or by anyone. Recognition also works better on clear, close, daylight photos than on dark, distant or cluttered ones. When Snoutbook is unsure, it says so rather than guessing at you.

How sure is a match?

A strong match, a possible match, and “here's the closest dog we found, but we're not sure” are three different screens, and you always know which one you are looking at. Treat any match as a strong hint, not proof.

Why does it ask for location?

To tell apart look-alike dogs in different neighbourhoods. Only a rough area — about a 5 km square — is ever stored, never your precise position, and never a history. Location is optional and everything works without it. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from inside the app at Settings → Delete my account, or by email. See deleting your account for exactly what is removed and what stays.

A dog's information is wrong.

The person who registered a dog can edit it. If that isn't you, or the dog's registrant is gone, email us and we will fix or remove it.

A note on safety

The interaction advice in Snoutbook is general guidance, not veterinary or medical advice. Community dogs are animals, not exhibits. Do not approach a dog that is eating, sleeping, injured, or with puppies. If you are bitten or scratched, wash the wound and see a doctor — in India, contact the nearest anti-rabies clinic immediately.
